Our first method ranked on raw counts and penalised the Accord for being popular. Switching to share inverts the result — and independently surfaces the Hyundai/Kia Theta II engine failure episode from public data, without being told to look for it.

Limits and next

What I would not claim

Known weaknesses

  • Price model has 21% median error — published, not buried. App shows a band, not a number.
  • The 1.35× out-of-warranty multiplier is the biggest judgement call, and it is an assumption.
  • Share-based risk cannot see a defect an entire segment shares.
  • The Critic is itself an LLM: a strong filter, not a proof.

Next, in order

  1. Finish user testing.
  2. Discount defects already remedied free under recall.
  3. Cache dossiers per vehicle — collapses marginal cost.
  4. Widen the depreciation fit beyond 581 listings.
  5. VIN-level recall matching.
